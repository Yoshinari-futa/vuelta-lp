Craft Cocktail Bar — Hiroshima, Japan

Bar VUELTA is a craft cocktail bar in Otemachi, central Hiroshima — a one-minute walk from the Chuden-mae tram stop and walking distance from the Peace Memorial Park. Opened in March 2026, it turns the city itself into cocktails: okonomiyaki served clear, Seto Inland Sea oysters in a gin sonic, Miyajima's momiji manju as a dessert cocktail. Eight counter seats, standing room for about eight more, an English menu, and the lights stay on until 2 AM.

Bar VUELTA（ブエルタ）は広島市中区大手町のクラフトカクテルバー。中電前電停から徒歩1分、平和記念公園から徒歩圏。お好み焼きや牡蠣、もみじ饅頭など、広島の味をカクテルに再構築しています。VUELTAはスペイン語で「再会」。旅行者と地元の人がひとつのカウンターで出会う店です。

Story Angles For Editors

Angle 01

Hiroshima's soul food, served clear

The OKONOMIYAKI #3 (1,000 yen) rebuilds the city's post-war soul food as a cocktail: shochu layered with Otafuku sauce and cabbage water, milk-washed until silky and clear, finished with bacon and aosa seaweed. Now in its third recipe, it tastes — to most guests' surprise — properly of okonomiyaki.

Angle 02

A city you can drink

The Hiroshima Cocktail Journey (4,000 yen) tells the region in three acts: Tipsy Crane, a Negroni built on Hiroshima sake and named for the origami crane; Shell We?, gin with house dashi rice vinegar in honour of the Seto Inland Sea's oysters; and The OKONOMIYAKI. The back bar leans local — SAKURAO gin from Hatsukaichi, Taketsuru sake, Hiroshima lemon — and Don't Feed the Deer turns Miyajima's momiji manju into a dessert cocktail.

Angle 03

Welcome back meets nice to meet you

VUELTA is Spanish for "return." The bar sits between the Peace Memorial Park and the nightlife district, and the counter regularly seats travellers and locals side by side. The menu is written in English, walk-ins are welcome, solo guests are common, and every Tuesday is Taco Tuesday — any tacos on the menu, two pieces for 500 yen.
